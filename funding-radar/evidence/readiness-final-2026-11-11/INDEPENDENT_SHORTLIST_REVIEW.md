## Criteria and decision rule

The [saved official Funding Radar brief](../../docs/BRIEF.md) asks for a ranked investor list with investment proof, typical cheque/stage, the named person responsible for leading the deal and a real contact route. It does not specify a minimum number of investors, require every portfolio investor, demand the job title Partner, require sole negotiating authority or prescribe one exact attribution verb.

A transaction-specific first-person leadership statement plus company/founder corroboration can establish the responsible investment lead even when the speaker uses a collective pronoun. Explicit professional mapping of named people to the firms leading a particular round can also be usable with corroboration. A bare quote, board appointment, portfolio listing, team title or generic practice leadership remains insufficient alone. Overall round-leading firm and the responsible person within a participating firm must stay separate.

## Read-only ranking code review

1. The digest now includes the parsed startup alongside description, window, original observation digest and company review evidence. Changing only parsed stage invalidates the prior ranking review with `RANKING_IDENTITY_MISMATCH`.
2. Uncertain geography now stays conditional and returns `approach_ready:false`, rather than obtaining an unconditional approach-ready flag from stage fit alone. Confirmed fit is still a source-reviewed judgment, not a funding or admission guarantee.

The present guards also reject pre-collection/future review times, unsupported or duplicated company references, unrelated investment proof URLs, reversed ranges, unsafe source URLs, quote-only attribution codes and score overrides. Sources still require substantive human review: structural validation cannot establish that the page says the supplied claim. Source/reference presence must not be described as automatic semantic verification.
