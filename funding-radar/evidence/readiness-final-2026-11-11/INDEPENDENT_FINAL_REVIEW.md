## General-input boundary and the fourth investigation

The generic path in [recent.ts](../../src/recent.ts), [recent-live.ts](../../src/recent-live.ts), [research-session.ts](../../src/research-session.ts) and [server.ts](../../src/server.ts) does not select a canned research result from a startup-description whitelist. Saved case IDs are navigation and replay fixtures; fresh research accepts a new description and explicit request identity. The limited local parser can reduce scoring precision for unsupported country, sector or stage vocabulary, but it does not discard the full description sent to research.

The [genuine live receipt](live-test.json) records one completed request through the real UI, result view and downloads. The preserved [export](../recorded-investigations/fresh-security-export.json), [company review](../recorded-investigations/fresh-security-review.json) and [investor review](../recorded-investigations/fresh-security-ranking.json) reconstruct as:

- Nineteen original provider rows, sixteen parser-retained recent claims, seventeen independently reviewed recent companies, two product-fit exclusions and zero ordinary-web company additions.
- A corrected Zania period beside the contradictory preserved raw month, rather than an edited provider receipt.
- One scored SignalFire record, with the responsible investment person, dated seed-policy range and programme-specific founder route qualified by their sources.
- SignalFire score 80 but `approach_ready: false`: AI-native eligibility is not established by the startup description, and current programme intake is unconfirmed. No other reviewed recommendation transfers from the original cases.

This is evidence for **generic research dispatch and one observed successful collection**, followed by explicit human review. It is not evidence of automatic company-fact verification or automatic complete investor enrichment. A future description can legitimately yield insufficient evidence or no suitable documented investor; the product must retain that state instead of manufacturing a positive result. The independent ranking function is generic over a bound review, but review creation is an operational dependency, not an automatic capability demonstrated here.
