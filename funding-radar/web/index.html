THE METHOD

A shorter list.
A longer paper trail.

Research is useful when you can see how it was reached. TinySet discovers company rounds. A separate source review checks what those pages actually establish.

  1. 01

    Describe the startup

    Product, funding stage and country shape the brief. Preparing a new plan is local and free of provider calls.

  2. 02

    Find comparable rounds

    TinySet researches company-round rows from the live web. Four genuine investigations are saved with original receipts. The first three runs remain intact.

  3. 03

    Check, then shortlist

    Review dates, investment links and fit. A commenting executive is not automatically the deal lead. Unknown cheques and contact routes stay unknown.

  4. 04

    Take the evidence with you

    Export JSON or CSV with original provenance, dated review findings and unresolved questions. No investor outreach is sent.
