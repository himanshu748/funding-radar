## General live-input path

Saved reads and `POST /api/plan` are local and free of provider operations. A separate UI/API accepts a new brief and explicitly starts bounded research only when both live flags and the authorized private grant are enabled. Defaults remain off. The job lifecycle provides request identity, status polling, cancellation requests, completed-result inspection and JSON/CSV exports. Repeating an identical request identity returns the same job rather than starting a new one. A failed or uncertain request is not automatically retried.

New job records and in-session results live in server memory; export deliberately before a restart. Bundled recorded exports and attached reviews are separate on-disk snapshots. A restored browser reference cannot recover a session lost by the server. Cancellation requests do not promise immediate remote termination or a refund, and uncertain remote state must be reconciled before further work. [Configuration and full lifecycle](LIVE_RESEARCH.md).

A completed provider job would establish a returned result, not independent semantic verification. New findings begin with source review pending, zero independently reviewed companies and no inherited approach-ready investor promotion. The saved-case counts and source reviews remain historical snapshots. The genuine authorized test completed and its real UI/result plus JSON/CSV downloads were confirmed. A separate later company review supports seventeen comparable companies; a subsequent SignalFire review supplies a conditional watchlist record, while other raw investor facts remain unreviewed. This proves one observed general-input journey, not the accuracy of every future result or automatic investor verification.

Optional transport diagnostics record only request stage, method, HTTP status and a constrained outcome/error class. They withhold credentials, headers, request/response bodies, arbitrary URLs, balances and provider identities. Observers do not change request outcomes or add retries. The [earlier request-contract audit](../evidence/completion-2026-11-11/request-contract-audit.json) found no clear documented request-shape mismatch; it did not identify the old failure's cause or establish a repaired live path.
