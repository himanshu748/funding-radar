# Funding Radar requirement check

The authenticated [Funding Radar brief and rulebook](https://www.tinybounties.com/crm?view=board) and the actual claim form were checked on 11 November 2026. This is a concise interpretation for the review package, not an organizer approval.

The build must accept a startup description including product, stage and country; find at least ten recent comparable companies; and explain a ranked investor shortlist. Each shortlisted investor needs investment proof, a scoped typical cheque range and stage, named deal responsibility and a real founder contact route. Demonstrate three different startups, use TinySet meaningfully and offer reusable output. Accuracy, startup-specific ranking, original work and general input handling are separate criteria.

The actual form requires a build URL, LinkedIn URL and X URL. Notes allow 2,000 characters. Media is optional, with up to five files at 25 MB each. The rulebook also requires a TinyFish Discord showcase share. The rulebook says X is optional while this form requires it; prepare both social links.

The technical cutoff is 17 November 2026 at 23:59 America/Los_Angeles, equivalent to 18 November at 13:29 Asia/Kolkata. Registration in the Students or Ambassadors program and individual ownership are required. Submission and approval remain separate, and the owner makes the final prize-entry click.
